About the job

We are seeking a talented and creative Web Designer & Developer to join our team full-time. The ideal candidate is skilled in both design and development, capable of turning ideas into visually appealing, user-friendly, and high-performing websites. This role requires strong technical expertise, attention to detail, and the ability to collaborate across teams to bring digital experiences to life.

Key Responsibilities

Design

  • Create modern, responsive website layouts, UI components, and digital assets.
  • Develop design concepts, wireframes, prototypes, and user journeys.
  • Ensure brand consistency across all digital platforms.

Development

  • Build and maintain websites using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern frameworks.
  • Optimize performance, accessibility, and cross-browser compatibility.
  • Collaborate with designers and stakeholders to deliver projects on time.

Qualification & Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Design, or a related field (or equivalent experience).
  • 3+ years of experience in web design and front-end development.
  • Proficiency in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and at least one modern framework (e.g. React, Vue).
  • Strong portfolio demonstrating UI/UX and development work.
  • Experience with responsive design and cross-browser testing.
